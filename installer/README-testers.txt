Golf Analyst 4.00 - for testers
===============================

Thank you for trying Golf Analyst!

What it is
----------
Golf Analyst keeps your golf scores and works out your Handicap Index the way
the World Handicap System does. It shows your statistics course by course, your
best rounds, how you play every hole, and it prints a handicap report, a log of
your rounds and a scorecard to take with you. Angelo Mileto first wrote it in
1991 on the Mac; this is the new Java version.

Installing it on Windows
------------------------
1. Double-click GolfAnalyst-4.0.0-testcopy.exe.
2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the program is not
   signed yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. Read the notice and credits, click Next, and choose whether you would like a
   shortcut on your desktop. No administrator password is needed: it installs
   just for you.
4. Start it from the Start menu: Golf Analyst.

On a Mac or Linux, use the zip instead and read its README.txt (you need Java 17
or newer).

Your data
---------
The program starts with made-up sample data, so there is something to look at
straight away. The first time you save something it offers to make your own
data folder, which is Documents\Golf Analyst. Close the program and open it
again, and from then on it uses your own folder. Your rounds, courses and
settings all live there, with the backups the program makes for you.

Uninstalling Golf Analyst (Settings, Apps, Golf Analyst, Uninstall) removes the
program only. It never removes Documents\Golf Analyst or your settings.

If something goes wrong
-----------------------
- Write down what you were doing just before it happened.
- Take a screenshot (on Windows press Windows + Shift + S).
- Look at the bar along the bottom of the window: it says what the program is
  doing, and it says so when a data file could not be read.
- Send these to Angelo. Every bit helps.

Have fun, and good golfing!
