Editing a Round - Help

Typed a 5 where you meant a 4? Here is how to put it right.

1. Find the round. Press Edit Round on the Home screen (or Rounds) and use the arrows at the bottom to step to the round. The Show box at the top can narrow it down to one course, one set of tees, or one kind of round on those tees (18 holes, front nine or back nine).

2. Press Edit. The title at the top now starts with Edit, and the boxes you can change turn white. The course, the tees and the holes stay as they are.

3. Make your changes. Click the box you want and type over it. The cursor moves on just like it does when you enter a round, and the F and S keys still check Fwy and Sand. Everything at the bottom and on the right is worked out again as you type, including what the round does to your handicap.

4. Press Save. Only this round's line in rounds.tsv is replaced. Your other rounds and any notes in the file are left alone. Your handicap and statistics are recalculated immediately.

Changed your mind? Press Cancel and the round goes back to how it was.

# A few things worth knowing

Historical: You can check or uncheck the Historical box at any time. A historical round never counts toward your Handicap Index. See Home Info for how Include historical rounds affects statistics.

A safety copy: Every time you save an edit, a copy of rounds.tsv as it was goes into a folder of its own inside the backups folder in your data folder, named with the date, the time and what was done (for example backups\2026-10-29_143012_edit-round). If an edit went wrong, close the program, copy the files from that folder back over the ones in your data folder, and open the program again. The program keeps the 20 newest of each kind, or as many as Backups to keep says in Settings.

Leaving with unsaved changes: If you press an arrow, Cancel or the Home button after changing something, the program asks "Discard your changes?" Say No to go back and press Save, or Yes to leave the round as it was.

The wrong course: The course and tees cannot be changed here. If you entered a round on the wrong course, enter it again on the right one, then delete the wrong one (see below).

# Deleting a round

1. Open the round for editing. Find it with Edit Round and the arrows, then press Edit.

2. Press Delete round. It is the small button under Historical, and you only see it while you are editing.

3. Check it is the right round. The first question names the round by its number, course, tees, date and score, as it is saved; anything you typed in the boxes is ignored. Press Keep it to stop, or Delete... to go on.

4. Type DELETE. The OK button is enabled only once the word is typed (capitals or not). Press OK, or Cancel to keep the round.

Only that round's line is taken out of rounds.tsv. Your other rounds, your notes in the file and the round numbers are left alone. Your handicap and statistics are recalculated immediately, and the screen shows the round before it (or the Home screen, if it was your only round). The bar along the bottom gives the name of the backup folder.

Getting a deleted round back: The backup folder is named with the date, the time and delete-round, as described above. To undo the delete, close the program, copy the files from that folder back over the ones in your data folder, and open the program again. Any round you saved after the delete has to be entered again.
