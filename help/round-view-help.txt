Looking at Your Rounds - Help

Here is how to look through your rounds.

# Getting here
Press Rounds on the Home screen. It opens at your most recent round.

# Moving around
Use the four arrow buttons at the bottom. The two outer arrows jump to the oldest and the newest round. The two inner ones step back and forward one round at a time. The bar along the bottom tells you where you are, for example Round 3 of 12.

# Looking at just one course
Open the Show box at the top and pick a course, or one set of tees on a course. The arrows then step through only those rounds. Pick All rounds to go back to everything. If the list has only one choice, it is because you have only played one set of tees.

# Watching your handicap change
Start at the oldest round, press Next again and again, and watch Index After. It shows your index right after each round was added.

# If you find a mistake
Step to the round and press Edit, at the bottom right. The boxes open so you can fix the date, PCC, scores, putts, fairways and sand, and Save puts the round back with your changes. The ? button on that screen walks you through it.

# Entering a new round
Press Close, or the Home button, to go back to the Home screen and then press New Round.

# Leaving this screen
Press Close or the Home button.
