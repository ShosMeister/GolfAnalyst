Editing a Round - Info

This is a round you have already saved, opened so you can fix it. The title at the top starts with Edit, followed by the round's number, the course and the tees, so you always know which round you are changing. The white boxes are the ones you can change. The grey boxes are worked out for you as you type.

# Across the top

Course / tee and Holes: Where and how much you played. They are locked while you edit, because the round belongs to that course and those holes. If you picked the wrong course when you entered it, the simplest fix is to enter the round again on the right course.

PCC: The day's Playing Conditions Calculation, normally 0. You can change it.

# The hole-by-hole tables

Score and Putt: The score and putts of each hole you played. You can change any of them.

Fwy and Sand: Whether you hit the fairway from the tee and whether you were in a bunker. Click them, or type F or S in the Putt box, to tick or untick them.

# The column on the right and the form at the bottom

These show the round as it would be with your changes: the eagles to others, the totals, fairways, sand saves, greens in regulation and two-putts, and what the round does to your handicap. Date: The day you played. You can change it.

# The buttons at the bottom right

Save: Puts your changes in rounds.tsv in place of the round as it was. Only that round's line is changed. Before saving, the file as it was is copied to rounds.tsv.bak in your data folder, so a bad edit can always be undone. Save is only on when every hole you played is complete and correct.

Cancel: Goes back to the round as it was, without saving.

# The arrows and the bar at the bottom

The four arrows still step through your rounds. If you have changed something and not saved it, the program asks "Discard your changes?" before it moves, and so do Cancel and the Home button. The bar along the bottom tells you what is still missing or wrong.
