Editing a Round - Info

This is a round you have already saved, opened so you can fix it. The title at the top starts with Edit, followed by the round's number, the course and the tees, so you always know which round you are changing. The white boxes are the ones you can change. The gray boxes are worked out for you as you type.

# Across the top

Course / tee and Holes: Where and how much you played. They are locked while you edit, because the round belongs to that course and those holes.

PCC: The day's Playing Conditions Calculation, normally 0. If your handicap record shows one for this round, you can type it here.

# The hole-by-hole tables

Score and Putt: Your score and putts for each hole you played. You can change either one.

Fwy and Sand: Whether you hit the fairway from the tee and whether you were in a bunker.

# The column on the right and the frame under the tables

These show the round as it would be with your changes: the Eagles-to-Others counts, the totals, fairways, sand saves, greens in regulation and two-putts, and what the round does to your handicap.

Date: The day you played. You can change it.

Historical: Check this for a historical round. It never counts toward your Handicap Index; see Home Info for how historical rounds affect statistics. You can check or uncheck it.

Delete round: The small button under Historical, there only while you edit. It removes this round from your rounds, but only after two questions: the first names the round (its number, course, tees, date and score) and the second asks you to type DELETE. A copy of rounds.tsv as it was is saved in a dated folder in the backups folder before the round is removed. The Help explains how to restore it.

# Save and Cancel, at the right end of the frame under the tables

Save: Puts your changes in rounds.tsv in place of the round as it was. Only that round's line is changed. A copy of rounds.tsv as it was is saved in a dated folder in the backups folder before the change; the Help explains how to put it back. Save is enabled only when every hole you played is complete and correct.

Cancel: Goes back to the round as it was, without saving.

# The arrows and the bar at the bottom

The four arrows still step through your rounds. The bar along the bottom tells you what is still missing or wrong.
