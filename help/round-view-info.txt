Looking at Your Rounds - Info

This screen shows one saved round at a time, exactly as it was saved. Nothing on it can be changed, which is why every box is grey.

At the top

The title across the top is the round's ID number, the course and the tees, for example 00031:Sample Links:White.

Show: Which rounds the arrow buttons step through: all of them, all the rounds on one course, or all the rounds on one set of tees. Only courses and tees you have played are listed, and the number after each one is how many rounds it has.

Course / tee: The course and tees of this round, with the ID number of that set of tees in front.

Holes: Whether it was 18 holes, the front nine or the back nine. Holes that were not played are greyed out.

PCC: The Playing Conditions Calculation that was used. It is almost always 0.

The hole-by-hole tables

Hole, Par, Putt and Score are what they say. A check in Fwy means you hit the fairway from the tee on that hole. A check in Sand means you were in a bunker on that hole. Out and In are the totals for holes 1 to 9 and 10 to 18.

The column on the right

Rating: The Course Rating and the Slope Rating for the holes that were played. If it says "no rating" the round counts in your statistics but not in your handicap.

Eagles, Birdies, Pars, Bogeys, DBogeys, Others: How many holes of each kind the round had. Others means triple bogey or worse.

The form at the bottom

Date, Score and Putts: When the round was played and its totals.

Fairway: Fairways hit out of the par 4s and par 5s that were played.

Sand Saves: Times you saved par or better out of the holes where you were in the sand.

Greens In Reg: Holes where the strokes before your first putt (your score minus your putts) were no more than par minus 2.

2-Putt: Holes where you took two putts or fewer.

18-Hole Diff: The round's score differential, which is what your handicap is built from. For a nine-hole round it is the nine-hole differential plus an expected score for the other nine holes, based on your index on that day. It shows "pending" for a nine-hole round played before you had a Handicap Index, until you have played 54 holes.

Index After: What your Handicap Index was right after this round was added.

The four arrows and the bar at the bottom

The arrows are First, Previous, Next and Last round, within whatever you chose in Show. An arrow is greyed out when there is nowhere to go. The bar along the bottom says which round you are on and how many there are in your choice, for example Round 3 of 12.

Close goes back to the Home screen.
