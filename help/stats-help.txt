Stats Board - Help

Here is how to use the Stats Board.

# Getting here

Press Stats Board on the Home screen. The Home button at the top takes you back. Whether old (historical) rounds are counted here is set by the Include historical rounds box on the Home screen.

# Picking a row

Click a row and it is highlighted in cream with a gold outline, so it is easy to follow across all the columns. It stays highlighted until you click another one. The Up and Down arrow keys move the highlight one row at a time. They step straight over the Nine-hole rounds band.

# Seeing the rounds behind a row

Double-click a row, or press Enter on the highlighted one, and the Rounds screen opens at the newest round on that course and set of tees, with the Show box already set to them. The arrows there step through every round on those tees: 18-hole rounds, front nines and back nines together. Press Close there to come back to the Stats Board, with your sort and your highlighted row just as you left them.

# Sorting

Click any heading to sort by that column, smallest first (or A to Z for the course names). Click it again to turn the order round. A small triangle in the corner of the heading shows which column is sorted and which way. The 18-hole rows and the nine-hole rows are sorted separately, and the two total rows at the top never move. A dash always goes to the bottom, whichever way you sort. Your highlighted row stays highlighted wherever it moves to.

The program remembers your sort while it is open, so you can go and look at some rounds and come back to the same order. It starts sorted by course name each time you open the program.

# Reading the gold

Look for the courses with no gold at all: those are the ones to work on. The Info says exactly how the gold is chosen.

# The faint writing

You cannot click the faint writing or the blank rows below.

# When there are a lot of courses

The list scrolls once there are more rows than fit on the screen. Use the scroll bar on the right, its arrows, or the mouse wheel. The headings and totals stay where they are at the top.
