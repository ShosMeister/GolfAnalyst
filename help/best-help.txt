Best Round - Help

Here is how to use the Best Round screen.

# Getting here

Press Best Round on the Home screen. It opens on the course and tees of the round you played most recently, showing your best single round there.

# Choosing the course

Open the Course / tee box at the top and pick another course or set of tees. The screen changes straight away.

# Single or Composite

Click Single to see your lowest real round on those tees, exactly as you played it.

Click Composite to see your best score on every hole put together into one round. It is a fun way to see your potential: what you are capable of shooting on that course and those tees. Every hole on it is one you have really played that well, just not all on the same day. Compare its total with your best single round in the bar along the bottom. The gap is how many shots you are leaving out there.

# Leaving

Press Close, or the Home button, to go back to the Home screen. There is nothing to save here, so you can leave whenever you like.
