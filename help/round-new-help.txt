Entering a Round - Help

Here is how to enter a round, step by step. Have your scorecard in front of you.

1. Choose the course and tees. Open the Course / tee list at the top and pick the tees you played. If your course is not in the list it has not been added yet. Go back to Home and press New Course to add it.

2. Say how many holes you played. For a full round leave Holes at 18 holes. If you only played the front nine or the back nine of an 18-hole course, choose Front nine or Back nine and the other holes gray out. A nine-hole course has nothing to choose.

3. Check the date. It starts as today. If you are entering an older round, change it. You can type 2026-10-22 or 10/22/2026.

4. Enter the scores. The cursor starts in the Score box of hole 1. Type the score for each hole, one after the other. The cursor moves down the holes by itself, over to hole 10 after hole 9, and on to the putts after the last hole. A score from 2 to 9 moves on right away. If you type a 1 the cursor waits, because it could be a hole in one or the start of a score like 10 or 12. Press Enter to accept the 1, or type the second digit. Only digits can be typed in these boxes. If you picked up on a hole, type the number you wrote on your card. To fix a box, click it and type the new digit over the old one.

5. Enter the putts. After the last score the cursor goes on to the Putt box of hole 1. Type the putts for each hole in the same way, one digit each.

6. Watch the boxes as you go. The totals, the Eagles-to-Others counts and the fairway, sand, green and two-putt figures all update as you type. When every hole is filled in you also see the 18-Hole Diff and what your Handicap Index will be after this round.

7. Fix anything that is pink. A pink box means something cannot be right, for example putts that are not fewer than the score. The bar at the bottom says what to fix. Save stays disabled until every hole is filled in and nothing is wrong.

8. Press Save. The round is added to the end of rounds.tsv in your data folder. Your other lines and comments in the file are not touched. The screen then shows the saved round, locked, so you can check it. Press Close, or the Home button, to go back to Home.

If you change your mind, press Cancel. Nothing is saved. If you have already typed something, the program asks "Discard your changes?" first, so a slip of the mouse does not lose your card. It asks the same if you press the Home button or close the window.

# A few things worth knowing

The F and S keys are a shortcut, and you do not have to use them. If your scorecard has an F (fairway) or an S (sand) on the putts line, you can type that letter right in the Putt box. F checks or unchecks Fwy for that hole and S does the same for Sand. The letter is not typed into the box. Type it before the putts digit, because the cursor moves on to the next hole as soon as the digit goes in. A hole marked F2 on the card is typed F then 2, and a hole with both an F and an S is typed F, S, then the putts. If you do not mark F and S on your card, or you would rather use the mouse, just click the Fwy and Sand boxes. Either way works and you can mix them. The Fwy box is switched off on par 3s, so F does nothing there. Any other letter is ignored.

For the rule about what counts as a putt, see the Putt entry in New Round Info. In short, a stroke from the fringe is not a putt, even if you use your putter; a chip-in has 0 putts.

PCC: Leave it at 0 when you enter a round the day you play it. The handicap system works the PCC out overnight from everyone's scores, so the number does not exist until later. If your handicap record (GHIN, for example) then shows a PCC for the round, press Edit Round, type it in and Save.

Historical: Check this box for a historical round. It remains available in Rounds but never counts toward your Handicap Index. See Home Info for how Include historical rounds affects statistics.

Nine-hole rounds: If the Rating box says "no rating," the round is still saved and counts in your statistics, but not in your handicap. To add the ratings, go back to Home and press New Course, type the course name and ZIP, then fill in that nine's rating and slope (the Front 9 or Back 9 row) and press Save. Your handicap is worked out again as soon as the course is saved, so the round counts immediately. If you add the ratings by editing courses.tsv yourself instead, it counts the next time you start the program.
