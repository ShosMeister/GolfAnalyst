Entering a Round - Info

This is the screen for entering a new round. The white boxes are the ones you type in. The gray boxes are worked out for you as you type and cannot be changed. To look back at rounds you have already saved, use the Rounds button on the Home screen instead.

# Across the top

Course / tee: The course and tees you played, with the ID number of that set of tees in front. Every set of tees has its own number. The list comes from the file courses.tsv.

Holes: 18 holes, the front nine or the back nine. A nine-hole course has only one choice.

PCC: The Playing Conditions Calculation, from -1 to 3. It adjusts a round for a day when the course played harder or easier than normal, and it is almost always 0. Your handicap system works it out after the day's scores are in and shows it in your scoring record (a dash means 0). It is only used for the handicap.

# The hole-by-hole tables

Out holds holes 1 to 9 and In holds holes 10 to 18. Holes you are not playing are grayed out. Only digits can be typed in these boxes. In Putt, the F and S keys also work as shortcuts (see Help).

Hole: The hole number.

Par: Par for the hole, from the course.

Score: Your strokes on the hole.

Putt: How many putts you took, 0 to 9. A putt counts only when your ball is on the putting green. A stroke from the fringe or collar is not a putt, even if you use your putter; if you hole out from off the green, the hole has 0 putts.

Fwy: Whether you hit the fairway from the tee. It is switched off on par 3s, because on a par 3 you are aiming at the green.

Sand: Whether you were in a bunker at any time on the hole.

The Out and In boxes at the bottom of each table total the putts and scores for that half.

# The column on the right of the tables

Rating: The Course Rating and the Slope Rating of the tees you chose, for the holes you are playing. A nine-hole round on an 18-hole course needs front nine or back nine ratings in the course file to count toward your handicap. If it has none, the round still counts in your statistics and this box says "no rating".

Eagles, Birdies, Pars, Bogeys, DBogeys, Others: How many holes of each kind you have so far. Others means triple bogey or worse.

18-Hole Diff: A number that says how well you played this round once the difficulty of the course is taken into account. Lower is better. It is how far your score was from what a scratch golfer would shoot on those tees, adjusted for the slope, so a round on a hard course and a round on an easy one can be compared fairly. The program applies the net double bogey limit described in Home Info, so one disaster hole cannot wreck it. Your Handicap Index is built from your best ones. For a nine-hole round the program estimates the other nine holes, which is why it still says 18-Hole. It appears when every hole is filled in.

Index After: What your Handicap Index will be once this round is added. It also appears when every hole is filled in.

# The frame under the tables

Date: When you played. It starts as today. You can type it as 2026-10-22 or as 10/22/2026.

Score and Putts: The totals for the holes you have filled in.

Fairway, Sand Saves, Greens In Reg and 2-Putt: These figures are worked out for the round as you enter it. See Home Info for what each one means.

Historical: Check this for a historical round you want to keep for reference. Historical rounds never count toward your Handicap Index; Include historical rounds on the Home screen controls whether they count in your statistics.

Save and Cancel: Save adds the round to your data. Cancel throws away what you have typed. The ? button explains how to use them.

# Pink boxes and the bar at the bottom

A box turns pink when what is in it cannot be right. The bar along the bottom of the window says what is wrong, or what is still missing.
