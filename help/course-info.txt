New Course - Info

This is the screen for adding a course and up to four sets of tees. The white boxes are the ones you type in. The grey boxes are either worked out for you or are not open yet.

Across the top

Course and Zip: The name of the course and its 5-digit zip code. These come first. The tees stay grey until both are in. If that course is already in the program (the capitals and extra spaces do not matter, but the zip must be the same), the bottom bar tells you which tees it already has, and the tees you fill in here are added to it.

City and State: Where the course is. State is two capital letters, for example SC. For a course that is already in the program they are filled in for you and cannot be changed here.

Nine-hole course: Tick it for a course that only has nine holes, like the ones Spark Golf plays. Holes 10 to 18 and the front nine and back nine boxes disappear, and the stroke index runs from 1 to 9.

The tees

There are four columns, one for each set of tees. They start out named Ladies, Beginner, Intermediate and Expert, but you can call them anything (Blue, White, Gold...). Each name can only be used once on a course.

Rating and Slope: The USGA Course Rating and Slope Rating from the scorecard for these tees. A tee with no rating is skipped when you save, so you only fill in the tees you want.

Front 9 and Back 9: The nine-hole rating and slope of the front nine and the back nine. They are on newer scorecards and on the USGA's course rating website. You only need them if you want a nine-hole round played on this 18-hole course to count toward your handicap. Leave them blank otherwise, but if you fill one in you need its slope too.

Yards: The total yardage. You can leave it blank.

Par: The total par of the tee, worked out as you type the pars below. It is a handy check against the scorecard.

Par and SI for each hole: The par of the hole (3, 4, 5 or 6) and its stroke index, the hole handicap number on the scorecard (1 is the hardest hole). Each number from 1 to 18 is used once.

Nothing is copied from one tee to another. If a scorecard does not give a rating for a set of tees, leave it out rather than guess, because a made-up rating would throw off your handicap.

Across the bottom

Save adds the tees to courses.tsv and takes you back Home. Cancel goes back Home without saving anything.
