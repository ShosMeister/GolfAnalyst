New Course - Info

This is the screen for adding a course and up to four sets of tees. It is laid out like a scorecard: the captions run down the wooden frame on the left, and each set of tees has its own frame to the right. The white cells are the ones you type in. The cell with the cursor has a gold outline. The grey cells are worked out for you.

Across the top

Course and Zip: The name of the course and its 5-digit zip code. If that course is already in the program (the capitals and extra spaces do not matter, but the zip must be the same), the bottom bar tells you which tees it already has, and the tees you fill in here are added to it.

City and State: Where the course is. State is two capital letters, for example SC. For a course that is already in the program they are filled in for you and cannot be changed here.

Nine-hole course: Tick it for a course that only has nine holes, like the ones Spark Golf plays. Holes 10 to 18 and the Front 9 and Back 9 rows disappear, and the stroke index runs from 1 to 9.

The tees

There are four frames, one for each set of tees. They start out named Ladies, Beginner, Intermediate and Expert, but you can call them anything (Blue, White, Gold...). Each name can only be used once on a course.

Rating / Slope: The USGA Course Rating and Slope Rating from the scorecard for these tees. A tee with no rating is skipped when you save, so you only fill in the tees you want.

Front 9 and Back 9: The nine-hole rating and slope of the front nine and the back nine. They are on newer scorecards and on the USGA's course rating website. You only need them if you want a nine-hole round played on this 18-hole course to count toward your handicap. Leave them blank otherwise, but if you fill in one you need the other too.

Yards / Par: The total yardage, which you can leave blank, and the total par, worked out as you type the pars below. It is a handy check against the scorecard.

Par and SI for each hole: The par of the hole (3, 4, 5 or 6) and its stroke index, the hole handicap number on the scorecard (1 is the hardest hole). Each number from 1 to 18 is used once.

The arrow next to the first tee's name copies its pars and stroke index numbers into the other tees, but only into cells that are still empty. Nothing else is ever copied. If a scorecard does not give a rating for a set of tees, leave it out rather than guess, because a made-up rating would throw off your handicap.

Across the bottom

The bar along the bottom always tells you the next thing to fill in or fix. When everything is in order it says Ready to save, and the Save button comes on. Save adds the tees to courses.tsv and takes you back Home. Cancel goes back Home without saving anything.
